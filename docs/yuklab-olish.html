Android'ga o'rnatish

  1. Yuklab oling — QR'ni skaner qiling yoki yuqoridagi tugmani bosing.
  2. Yuklangach faylni oching (bildirishnomada yoki "Yuklamalar" papkasida).
  3. So'ralsa "Noma'lum manbalardan o'rnatish"ga ruxsat bering — bu Play Store'dan tashqari ilovalar uchun.
  4. O'rnating, ochib Google orqali kiring — hammangiz bitta dunyoda o'ynaysiz.
